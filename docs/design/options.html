Renames, moves, deletions and conversions are proposed first, as a before and after. Applied changes are logged and can be undone; duplicates go to quarantine, never straight to deletion.

06

Fits every window size

Compactunder 900 px
Standard900–1440 px
Wideover 1440 px

Layouts reflow on their own: the sidebar folds into a top bar, the detail panel drops below, poster grids add or remove columns.

07

Light and dark, designed together

Dark mode lifts surfaces with lighter greys instead of inverting colours, so higher layers read as closer. Body text keeps at least 4.5:1 contrast in both.

08

Keyboard first, mouse friendly

CtrlKSearch or run anything
ArrowsMove through posters
SpacePause or resume the queue
CtrlZUndo the last change

Every action has a shortcut, the focus ring is always visible and click targets are at least 40 px, the Windows touch guideline.
